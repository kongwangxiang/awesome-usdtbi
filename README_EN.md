## Wallets

Your keys, your coins. Safety starts with the right wallet. Full set on the [wallet favorites page](https://www.usdtbi.com/favorites/qianbaogongju?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme-en).

- [MetaMask](https://metamask.io/): The default browser wallet across EVM chains.
- [Phantom](https://phantom.app/): Smooth multi-chain wallet that feels at home on Solana and with NFTs.
- [Bitget Wallet](https://web3.bitget.com/): Mobile Web3 wallet with a built-in DApp browser and market quotes.
- [Ledger](https://www.ledger.com/): Hardware wallet for keeping large balances offline.
- [Trezor](https://trezor.io/): Open-source hardware wallet with a strong transparency record.
- [Safe](https://safe.global/): Multisig vaults for teams and shared treasuries.
- [Rabby](https://rabby.io/): EVM wallet that auto-switches chains and flags risky transactions before you sign.

Hard rules: write your seed phrase on paper, never photograph it, never sync it to the cloud, and never send it to anyone. Keep a spending wallet separate from your savings wallet, and revoke leftover token approvals when a session is done. Any "support agent" who asks for your seed phrase is running a scam.

## FAQ

**Q: What is an exchange referral and why should I use one?**

An exchange referral returns a slice of your trading fees to you, and through these links a share also supports this site. Binance, OKX, Bitget, Bybit, and the rest bind that relationship while you register, and the binding then lasts for the life of the account. Skip the referral link and you pay full fees forever.

**Q: Can I add a referral code after registering?**

Almost never. Once the invite relationship is written at signup, most exchanges refuse to alter it, so a plain registration cannot be upgraded later. Your first click has to be the referral link, which is why this warning sits above the table.

**Q: How do I deposit safely and keep my bank card out of trouble?**

Start with a small test order in the exchange's P2P market, pick merchants with high completion rates, and keep your payment receipts. For on-chain deposits, copy the address and pick the network exactly as shown on the deposit page, then double-check the chain before you confirm. A wrong network loses the funds with no refund desk to call.

**Q: Is USDTBi free?**

Yes. Reading the site and this list costs nothing, and every tool listed here has a free tier or a free core use. The project only earns when you choose to open an exchange account through one of the referral links.
